Behind the worlds

I’m Joshua Pugh.

I’m a diesel technician, software developer, writer, and builder of things.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Software Engineering. When I’m not repairing heavy machinery, I build college-football prediction models, design games, create fictional worlds, and write stories—including The Cursed War and Ashen Throne.

Most of my projects begin with a simple question: What if I built it? Campaign notes become novels. Pieces of lore become interactive websites. Programming experiments become models, games, and playable experiences.

Away from the keyboard, I enjoy fishing, spending time with my family, playing games with my son, and listening to heavy metal.

“I believe stories should feel like places people can enter—and building the door is half the fun.”
