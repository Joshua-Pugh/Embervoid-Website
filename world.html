Kingdoms of Gursol

A continent held together by necessity.

Four powers surround a shared port, divided by mountains, old grievances, and lands whose former names have not been forgotten.

Northern kingdom

Ashlar

A mountain-bound realm whose capital, Windermere, stands above the Northern Pass. The Eastern Reach stretches beneath its authority into quieter, less certain country.

Western kingdom

Rainwynn

A fertile kingdom ruled from Aethelgard and scarred by seven years of vengeance. Its banners now face Kentmore across land that once belonged to Vareth.

Eastern kingdom

Azgoth

A disciplined power ruled from Dorskalla, where aid is measured, recorded, and never offered without a price.

Southeastern kingdom

Kentmore

Kentmore’s banners fly over conquered Vareth lands and older dwarven holdings, far beyond the kingdom’s southeastern heartland.

Ruined kingdom

Old Vareth

Once the land between Rainwynn and Kentmore. Its territory is divided now, but its roads, ruins, and memory remain.

Dwarven stronghold

Drakmorn

The last dwarven city still held in the northern Kentmore mountains—a place armies have reached and failed to break.

Crossroads

The Neutral Port

Where the kingdoms nearly meet, protected roads converge on a shared harbor. Trade, diplomacy, and river travel depend upon its continued neutrality.

To close the port would be to close a hand around the throat of Gursol.
— Common strategic saying
